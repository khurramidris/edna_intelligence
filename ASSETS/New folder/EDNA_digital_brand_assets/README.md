# EDNA — Digital Brand Assets

Prepared from the selected EDNA architectural-E concept. The original monogram and outlined wordmark are reused from the previous vector identity work, rather than redrawn with a different font.

## Folders

- **01_profile:** 1080x1080 branded social avatars in charcoal, ivory, and olive; crop-safe for circular DP presentation.
- **02_social_feed:** five editorial 1080x1350 brand posts and one square 1080x1080 post. These are brand-first identity images, not ads claiming real stock, products or dates.
- **03_social_stories:** two 1080x1920 story/reel covers.
- **04_linkedin:** 1128x191 company banner, 1584x396 personal banner, and 1200x628 editorial post.
- **05_website:** transparent horizontal and mobile logos in charcoal and ivory, white-on-dark footer logo, 750x1000 splash, 1920x720 editorial banner, 1200x630 website social-share image.
- **06_favicons:** 16, 32, 48, 96, 180, 192, 512 px icon PNGs, transparent-free SVG favicon, Apple touch icon, ICO.
- **07_brand_backgrounds:** seamless-look brand pattern references in ivory and olive, and core colour palette swatch sheet.

**Core colours:** charcoal #211C19, ivory #EEE7DD, sand #C8B6A1, stone #796B62, olive #494D43.

## Recommended usage

1. Instagram / Facebook profile photo: `01_profile/edna_profile_charcoal.png`
2. Website favicon: `06_favicons/favicon.ico` or `favicon_32.png`; Apple icon `apple_touch_icon_180.png`.
3. Website header on light background: `05_website/website_header_logo_charcoal_transparent.svg`
4. Website header on dark background: `05_website/website_header_logo_ivory_transparent.svg`
5. Footer on dark background: `05_website/website_footer_logo_ivory_transparent.svg`
6. LinkedIn company cover: `04_linkedin/linkedin_company_banner_1128x191.png`
7. Instagram grid: `02_social_feed` files, in any order, with consistent identity.

## Notes

All compositions are original editable SVG artwork plus export-ready PNGs. The standalone EDNA mark is vector, while secondary campaign headings in the SVG rely on local display and sans-serif fonts; PNGs guarantee appearance.
The logo/monogram remain conceptual: undertake trademark clearance and professional Bézier/optical refinements before claiming exclusive registered ownership.
The backgrounds are identity art, not photographs of real footwear or a claim about finished products.
